Heritage & Conservation

The Sonnenpfad Story

"Sonnenpfad" represents the path of the sun illuminating Africa's wilderness. Founded in Kampala, Uganda, we curate bespoke wildlife journeys across East Africa with deep respect for local communities, wildlife conservation, and exquisite comfort.

Born in the Pearl of Africa, Connecting the Entire East African Rift

Our company was founded with a singular conviction: that travel in Africa should be personal, unhurried, and genuinely impactful. We began as a specialist gorilla trekking company in southwestern Uganda's Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and have expanded to encompass cross-border safari circuits spanning the Serengeti, Masai Mara, Ngorongoro Crater, and the volcanic slopes of Rwanda.

Along with our safaris, we roast our own specialty Arabica coffee hand-picked on Mount Elgon, ensuring every guest experiences the complete aroma and warmth of East African hospitality.
