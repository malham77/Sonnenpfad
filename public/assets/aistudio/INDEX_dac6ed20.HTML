The Journey Begins Here

Where extraordinary landscapes meet remarkable wildlife

Uganda is a land where extraordinary landscapes, remarkable wildlife and rich cultures meet.

At Sonnenpfad Safaris Uganda Ltd, we create carefully planned journeys that bring travellers closer to the heart of the Pearl of Africa. From encounters with mountain gorillas in mist-covered forests to game drives across open savannahs and boat journeys along the Nile, every safari is designed around discovery, comfort and meaningful experiences.

Whether you are visiting Uganda for the first time, returning for another adventure or planning a private journey for your family or group, we help turn your travel ideas into an unforgettable African experience.

Experience Uganda Differently

More than a safari

From gorilla trekking to cultural encounters, discover the experiences that make Uganda unforgettable.

Gorilla Trekking

Face to Face with the Wild

Walk beneath an ancient forest canopy in search of one of the world's most remarkable primates. A gorilla trek is not simply another wildlife sighting — the forest journey, anticipation and eventual encounter combine to create an experience many travellers remember for the rest of their lives.

Chimpanzee Experiences

Into the Primate Forest

Discover the energy of Uganda's tropical forests while searching for chimpanzees and other primates. Combine your chimpanzee experience with birding, nature and photography for a deeper exploration of Uganda's forest ecosystems.

Wildlife Safaris

Africa in Motion

Watch elephants move across open landscapes, encounter buffalo, antelope and other wildlife, and search for predators while travelling through some of Uganda's most beautiful protected areas.

Boat Safaris

See Uganda from the Water

Some of Uganda's most memorable wildlife encounters happen from the water. Journey along iconic waterways while watching hippos, crocodiles, elephants and abundant birdlife along the banks.

Cultural Experiences

Meet the People Behind the Destination

Uganda is much more than its wildlife. Responsible cultural experiences give travellers opportunities to learn about local traditions, food, music, crafts and everyday life while respecting the communities being visited.

Into the Forest

Meet Uganda's Mountain Gorillas

Walk beneath the canopy of Uganda's ancient mountain forests and experience one of Africa's most remarkable wildlife encounters. A guided gorilla trek brings travellers into the natural habitat of these extraordinary primates while supporting conservation and responsible tourism.

Our Story

The sun path to discovery

Sonnenpfad means "sun path" — a name that reflects our belief that travel is a journey toward discovery.

Sonnenpfad Safaris Uganda Ltd was created to introduce travellers to the extraordinary landscapes, wildlife and cultures of Uganda through carefully designed safari experiences.

Our goal is simple: to help every traveller experience Uganda in a way that feels personal, authentic and unforgettable.

Every journey can be adapted around the traveller — from short wildlife escapes to extended expeditions combining primates, national parks, landscapes, culture and adventure.

Company history, founding dates and verified details will be added by the founders.

Responsible Tourism

Travel with Purpose

We believe that travel should enrich both the visitor and the visited. Responsible tourism means making choices that respect Uganda's natural and cultural heritage.

Every Sonnenpfad journey is designed with care for the places and communities we visit.

Wildlife

Respecting animals in their natural habitats and supporting conservation efforts.

Protected Areas

Supporting Uganda's national parks and the vital work of wildlife authorities.

Local Communities

Creating meaningful benefits for the people who call these landscapes home.

Natural Environments

Minimising our impact and encouraging sustainable travel practices.
