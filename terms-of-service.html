2. Safari Bookings & Primate Permit Allocations

  • All published prices on our website are denominated in Ugandan Shillings (UGX) unless a written custom invoice states otherwise.
  • Gorilla and chimpanzee tracking permits are issued exclusively by the Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) or Rwanda Development Board (RDB) on a first-paid basis and require written booking confirmation prior to permit reservation.
  • Wildlife viewing takes place in natural habitats; while guides select optimal tracking sectors, sightings of specific wild species cannot be guaranteed as an absolute certainty.

3. Age Restrictions & Parental / Guardian Consent

  • Website Forms & Orders: Individuals must be 18 years or older (or accompanied by explicit parent/guardian consent) to submit bookings, orders, or guest reviews on this website.
  • Minimum Park Permit Ages: Per UWA wildlife regulations, participants must be at least 15 years old for Mountain Gorilla trekking and at least 12 years old for Chimpanzee tracking.
  • Minors traveling on family game drives or launch cruises must remain under continuous adult guardian supervision.

4. Expedition Shop Orders & Delivery
