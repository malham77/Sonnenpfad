The Sonnenpfad Story

Founded in Kampala, Uganda, Sonnenpfad Safaris Uganda Ltd operates private 4x4 expeditions across Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda alongside direct Mount Elgon coffee sourcing.

Born in Uganda, Operating Across the East African Rift

Our company began with specialist mountain gorilla trekking logistics in southwestern Uganda and grew into custom cross-border savannah circuits reaching Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, Masai Mara, and Rwanda's Volcanoes National Park.

In parallel with safari operations, we source shaded peaberry Arabica coffee from smallholder growers on Mount Elgon so travellers and local coffee lovers enjoy traceable Ugandan harvests.
